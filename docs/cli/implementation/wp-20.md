### Steps

1. **Prerequisites.**
   - The user's Anki is running with companion 0.3.0 (`labCapabilities` reports `0.3.0`).
   - Ollama is certified: if `curl -s localhost:11434/api/version` differs from the last certification (0.40.1), run `target/lab/v3.sh doctor --ollama --certify`.
   - At least 200 GB is free.
   - Read the WP-19 known limits.
2. **Per-note facts.** For each note, record:
   - the Word field (text or image);
   - the picture filenames;
   - whether each picture is an illustration or a dictionary or text screenshot — look at each file under `~/.local/share/Anki2/User 1/collection.media/`;
   - the audio files;
   - the Extra text;
   - the card IDs with their reps, interval and due date (cardsInfo), saved before any write.
3. **Pilot five notes** (two blue, two purple, one with a screenshot picture), one at a time, using the WP-19 single-note flow:
   - `target/lab/v3.sh --set "purposes.japanese_vocab.fields=$(cat target/lab/wp19/pw-fields-v3.json)" --set "purposes.japanese_vocab.source_model=2. Picture Words" vocab revamp --note-id N`;
   - `target/lab/wp19/gen.sh "$PWD/target/lab/v3.sh" PLAN SENSE_WORD` (resolves pre-generation reviews, runs certified generation, prints content);
   - read the generated content yourself and reject wrong facts with `LAB_REJECT=nuance` (or `usage`, `collocations`), plus the other driver knobs: `LAB_ARCHIVE_FILES="Screenshot (N).png|…"` for screenshot pictures, `LAB_IMAGE_PICK=digestprefix|dictionary` to choose a candidate, `LAB_AUDIO_PICK=decline` to keep the user's own recording;
   - `target/lab/wp19/apply.sh CLI PLAN --accept-schema-change`;
   - render the backs with `target/lab/wp19/preview2.py` + `shot.py` and look at them.
   - Ask the user to review the pilot in Anki (`flag:4 or flag:7`, `edited:1`) before doing the rest.
4. **The rest as batches** with the WP-21 path: blue and purple in batches of about 10, one checkpoint per batch.
   - Every picture decision is made by looking at the image.
   - Every generated fact is read before it is verified; reject what is wrong.
5. **Verify after each batch:**
   - card IDs, reps, intervals and due dates are unchanged;
   - every `src=` and `[sound:]` reference exists in the collection media folder;
   - the model is v3 and the deck is unchanged.
6. **Hand-off for feedback.**
   - Write a review sheet as an artifact (a page): one row per note with the expression, what changed (pictures replaced or kept, audio, rejected facts), and anything you were unsure of.
   - Tell the user to search `flag:4 or flag:7` in the Anki browser.
   - Remind them to choose **Upload** on the next full sync.
7. **Feedback loop.**
   - Turn the user's comments into code fixes (render, prompt, parser) or per-note re-revamps.
   - Record them in a completion audit and an exhaustive **Known limits** section in this file.
   - Commit and push.

### Known limits — revisit after all packages

- **Full sync.** Every changed note type needs a full sync, and the user must choose **Upload**. A download would undo all 53 revamps and recreate nothing (WP-19 RI-11 limit).
- **Generated content quality.** `gemma4:12b` often produced wrong or empty phrases: collocations that repeat the word or its Japanese gloss, a near-synonym explained backwards, possessive forms such as 我の, own-wife versus other's-wife misuse, an invented figurative use (太陽に雲がかかる), and a nearby verb in place of the word (驚かす for 脅かす). The reviewer removed whole fields, because review is per field and not per item, so good collocations or examples were lost with bad ones. Generation differs between runs when inputs change (rollbacks and re-splits changed several cards' text).
- **Pictures.** Some picks are weak (12 rows are marked Check in the sheet). いらすとや search is the site's Blogger feed, ranked by title match only. Its terms allow personal use; redistributing a deck that contains many of its pictures needs a check. Commons by gloss rarely helps for abstract Japanese words. Search terms are chosen by the reviewer per item (`--set images.search_terms=...` on `plans regenerate --stage enrichment`).
- **Authored meanings.** Six meanings were written by the reviewer where Jisho has no exact entry: 副委員長, 折り目をつける, 迷惑がかかる, 太陽に雲がかかる, エンジンがかかる and 優勝がかかる. They come from the user's own glosses or general knowledge, not a dictionary.
- **OCR notes.** Expressions and readings of the phrase lists were typed by the reviewer from the OCR text; there is no typed path that turns a line like `X ( reading ) : gloss` into a unit. The Vietnamese glosses were read but not stored.
- **Vocabulary split.**
  - The template pairs words, readings and recordings by order only.
  - Pictures go to the anchor unless the reviewer moves them.
  - A recording shared by two readings (節) is not assigned.
  - Two notes with the same expression (脅かす おどかす/おびやかす, 節 ふし/せつ) exist side by side; Anki's duplicate check flags them.
  - Splits are applied per group with one checkpoint each, not through apply jobs.
- **Kana words.** The Spelling front of a kana word shows only the recording and meaning. A new sibling without its own recording (あたし, エンジンがかかる) gets no Spelling card.
- **Pronunciation.** Pronunciation keeps the user's text (肝 かん, though Jisho gives きも).
- **Large media.** The decoded-image limit is a global setting (raised to 64 MB in the user's config, default 10 MB). The companion still returns bytes only for files up to 1 MiB.
- **Checkpoints and rollbacks.** Some re-runs needed rollbacks (秘書 order, あたし Spelling, sibling tasks, the 奥さん pictures); each cost a checkpoint. 177 GB of checkpoints remain until pruned.
- **Batch review tooling.** It is lab-only (`target/lab/wp20/{batch.py,job.sh,multi.sh,show.py,verify.py}`); see WP-21 Known limits.
- **Release check (2026-10-09).** `release-check.py --msrv --release-build --benchmark` on a clean tree: every command passes, gates EV-01 and EV-03–EV-10, EV-12–EV-14 pass (all ten desktop scenarios, including the batch apply-job and vocabulary-split ones), EV-02 stays blocked, and EV-11 fails: `gemma4:12b` schema compliance was 52/55, 54/55 and 52/55 over three runs (bar 95%). Failures are incomplete examples and completions cut off at the output cap. Evidence: `docs/cli/evidence/release-2026-10-09`.
