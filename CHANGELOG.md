# Changelog

## Unreleased

Faster campaigns.

- **Keigo drill cards as text (WP-28)**: a grammar-deck note whose pages are a rephrasing drill (a plain sentence on the front, its business-keigo versions under "Answer" on the back) is reported as `DRILL_CANDIDATE` and can be rewritten in place as a `Linguist Drill v1` note with `plans convert-drill PLAN --template ITEM` and `--request FILE`. The note and its card are kept; the card becomes the drill's one `Production` card. The front shows the plain sentence with the source's reading hints as furigana and a tap-build board: the first polite version cut into bunsetsu chunks plus decoys made from the plain words the drill changes (もらった against ちょうだいした), shuffled with a daily seed, checked against every accepted version as you tap, with Undo. The back lists every accepted version with the polite parts highlighted and its own audio, the change notes and an English translation. The reviewer writes the drill from the pages, so no LLM call is spent; the audio stage voices each answer. `models install japanese_drill --apply` installs the note type; it needs companion 0.7.0, which accepts the drill fields. The binary accepts companions 0.3.0 to 0.7.0.
- **`models install|update japanese_drill`**: the first live run failed with `UNSUPPORTED_PURPOSE: japanese_drill`. The command's purpose argument shared its name with the global `--purpose` option, so it was also resolved as a configuration purpose, and a drill has no configuration preset. The argument is now separate; the other purposes behave as before.
- **Batches waiting for the agent are reviewed again under new rules**: after the Vietnamese-screenshot rule was withdrawn, batches 394 and 398 kept asking the agent for eight senses the policy now decides; a batch waiting for the agent was reviewed again only when its plan changed, and `campaign agent-done` did not change that. Each review now records a digest of the review policy and generation checks it ran with (`checked_rules`); a waiting batch whose digest differs from the running binary's is reviewed again once.
- **A tick says why batches wait**: seven approved batches sat for ten minutes of idle ticks (`"steps": []`), and nothing showed why: a step that waited (Anki or the store unavailable, drafting cooling down) was dropped from the tick report. `campaign run` now reports `waiting`, the last reason per batch that did not move, from the write lane and the prepare lanes.
- **A number takes its counter**: 二十六 (384) kept one example of three. The review rejected 二十六人 and 二十六歳 as compounds (a kanji after the word), and after three drafts generation stopped asking. A number of two or more numerals now stands alone before any counter.
- **A compound may be glossed with a later meaning**: 乗り場 (385) failed three drafts with `GENERATION_COLLOCATION_GLOSS`. Its meaning lists "place for boarding vehicles; bus stop; …", and バス乗り場 glossed "bus stop" was refused as naming the word itself. Only a gloss equal to the first meaning is refused now (インスタントラーメンを食べる "instant ramen" still is); a later, narrower meaning is what the compound adds.
- **Ready batches can be approved during a drafting cooldown**: a `start` while Codex cooled down wrapped the session up within seconds, because only approved, applying, verifying and merging batches held it, and approvals are refused once the campaign is paused. Ready batches waited until the next drafting window, though applying them needs no drafting. For `APPROVAL_SECONDS` (default 1200) after a start, ready batches now hold the session too, so the agent approves them and they are written.
- **A screenshot's first sense is kept**: 円 (371) got "circle" although the learner's Jisho screenshot and picture show yen. Coverage ranked the one-word "circle" above "yen (currency of Japan)", of which the screenshot shows one word in three. A first sense the screenshot shows now stays unless another sense matches more of its words.
- **A screenshot in the learner's language is read in dictionary order**: 引き落とす (382) got "to pull down"; its screenshot explains it in Vietnamese ("direct debit; pull down"), which no English sense matches. Leaving such senses to the agent (`SENSE_SCREENSHOT_UNREAD`) was tried and withdrawn the same day: most notes keep a Vietnamese dictionary page, half of batch 398 went to the agent (over the agent share, a pause), and dictionary order was right for all of them (鋼, 鉄, 国家, 望む). Known limit: a sense only such a screenshot names is caught by the spot check, not the policy.
- **`campaign requeue --note NOTE --why TEXT`**: notes applied before a fix could not go through the campaign again; requeue took only deferred notes. The adverb rule above had left 46 applied notes (常に, 必ず, 単に, 自由に…) with fewer than three examples. Named applied notes of done batches now move to a new batch and are prepared, reviewed and applied again; notes that are not applied ones are listed under `not_applied`.
- **A busy agent session is not a silent one**: the session wrapped up for a missing heartbeat (2938 s) while the agent was fixing findings in one long turn; the 15-minute check fires only between turns. The driver now counts a tool command the agent session started within `BUSY_SECONDS` (default 300) as a heartbeat; the session's long-running background watchers do not count.
- **A する verb is used when it conjugates**: 察する (425) kept one example of three. The review rejected 察して and 察した as sentences without the word: the stem it looked for was 察す, the word minus its last kana. A する or ずる verb's stem is now what comes before する (察), so its conjugated forms count.
- **A model at capacity is no failed draft**: a note of batch 421 failed three drafts with `Selected model is at capacity. Please try a different model.` and went to the agent as `GENERATION_FAILED`. The provider was busy for everyone for a moment; the same draft succeeds later. Such an error (at capacity, overloaded, temporarily unavailable) now makes the batch wait like a usage limit, without counting as a failed draft.
- **Two readings of one dictionary entry are one word**: 続柄 (415), read ぞくがら/つづきがら, asked for a split (`VOCAB_SPLIT_REQUIRED`). A split would have made two cards of one word. Jisho gives both readings in one entry of 続柄. Readings that all belong to one dictionary entry of the word no longer ask for a split; readings with entries of their own (おどかす / おびやかす) still do.
- **Careful mode ends at the top of its ramp only**: a careful run (one batch at a time, ramping up) lasted 8356 s before the Codex cap, and a run that long ended careful mode. The next start went straight to 20 batches ahead, 4 at a time, and spent 7% to 71% of the window in 37 minutes, 6 points past the cap. That run reached the cap after 2200 s, more than `FAST_CAP_SECONDS` (1800), so careful mode did not come back either. Careful mode now ends only when its ramp reaches the saved limits, and a cap within two hours (`FAST_CAP_SECONDS` 7200) of a full-speed start turns it on. `CALM_WINDOW_SECONDS` is gone.
- **A deferred note's draft holds no batch**: batch 419 was ready, then waited for the rest of the session once 当事者 was deferred. Deferring dropped the rejected example, so generation drafted the note again, met Codex's usage limit, and the review waited for the window to reset (`GENERATION_UNAVAILABLE`). A deferred note stays as it is in Anki: its drafts' outcomes, a usage limit included, are now ignored.
- **Requeue does not wait for a busy driver**: with 200 notes in flight, the driver's ticks held the campaign lease for minutes on end, and `campaign requeue` failed `LEASE_HELD_OR_OWNER_UNVERIFIED` after ten minutes of retries. Requeue took the lease only so that the batch number it writes into the old batch ("REQUEUED to batch N") matched the new batch. It now runs without the lease: each move is one versioned transaction, and a number a tick took meanwhile is corrected in the old batch.
- **同士, 自身 and the like follow any noun**: 当事者 (419) kept two examples of three and lost its collocations. The review rejected この問題は当事者同士で解決してください and 当事者同士 as a compound. A word now stands alone before 同士, 自身, 全員, 以外, 以上, 以下, 以内, 未満 or 以来.
- **A quantity goes before a verb**: 一口 (417) kept two examples of three and no collocations. The review rejected このパン、一口食べてみて and 一口食べる as a compound 一口食 (a kanji right after the word). A word starting with a numeral (一口, 一杯, 二度) now stands alone before a kanji followed by okurigana, which starts a verb. A kanji followed by a particle (一口目に) is still taken for a compound.
- **A verb is not first its auxiliary use**: 兼ねる (409) got "to be unable to" with examples お答えしかねます and 決めかねています, although the learner's note glosses it 兼 ("combine"). Jisho lists the suffix use (after a verb's -masu stem, usually written in kana) before "to serve two or more functions or roles". A first sense Jisho gives as a suffix after the -masu stem now gives way to the word's first sense that is not one.
- **A spelling takes the sense Jisho marks as its own**: 遭う (408) got 会う's "to meet; to encounter", and an example 山道で見知らぬ人に遭った ("met a stranger on a mountain path"). Jisho lists 会う, 逢う, 遭う and 遇う as one entry and notes its second sense, "to have (e.g. an accident); to suffer", as "esp. 遭う when in kanji". Among senses of the word's reading, the review now takes a sense whose note marks it "esp. <the word>" when no screenshot names one.
- **A sense restricted to the word's reading stays first**: 粒 つぶ (406) got "counter for small round objects" instead of "grain; bead; drop". The counter rule written for 杯 (はい took "sake cup", a sense of さかずき) swaps a one-kanji word's first sense for the counter when another reading shares it, and Jisho lists "grain" for つぶ, つび and つぼ. Jisho restricts that sense to those readings, つぶ among them. A first sense restricted to the word's own reading is now kept.
- **A quantity's bound ends its phrase**: 取引 (402) kept two examples of three. The review rejected この会社とは十年以上取引しています ("we have done business with this company for over ten years") as a compound 以上取引, as the compound rule took any kanji right before the word as part of it. A word after 以上, 以下, 以内, 未満, 以来, 程度 or 前後 now stands alone.
- **Reader comments in a screenshot are not the entry**: 祝い (400) got "congratulatory gift; present" although the learner's Mazii screenshot explains it as "lễ mừng; chúc mừng" (celebration, congratulation). Under the entry, the page lists readers' comments ("Có 9 ý kiến đóng góp"). One of them gave "moving gift, housewarming gift" in English, the only English words on the page, and so the only words that matched a sense. The sense review now reads a screenshot only up to that header (matched without diacritics, as OCR drops them).
- **A vocabulary split unit gets its dictionary entry**: after the split of 我々 / 私たち / 僕たち / 僕ら / 我ら (384), all five units kept an empty meaning and sense, and the batch waited for the agent. A split unit starts with no dictionary entry, and the campaign only drafted it (`plans generate --pending`), which writes no meaning. The review now runs the dictionary stage (`plans regenerate --stage dictionary --item-id`) for a split unit with no dictionary entry and an empty meaning, at most three times, as it does for an entry lost to a provider outage.
- **A vocabulary split drops what belonged to the whole note**: splitting 我々 / 私たち / 僕たち / 僕ら / 我ら (384) left every unit invalid (`INVALID_MEDIA_TYPE`, `EVIDENCE_TARGET_INVALID`): each unit starts from empty content, but kept the original's kanji stroke images and the evidence of its dictionary senses and examples. A vocabulary unit now drops the strokes and that evidence; enrichment looks its own word up again.
- **A kanji adverb before a kanji word is no compound**: 一応 (394) kept one example of seven drafted; 一応話せます was rejected as a compound 一応話 (the compound rule took any kanji right after a kanji-ending word as part of it). A word whose chosen sense Jisho labels an adverb now skips the compound rule, in examples and collocations.
- **An adverb before a verb is no compound**: the review rejected every example and collocation of 前もって and いつの間にか (378), three drafts each, so both reached approval with no examples. A word ending in kana was taken for a noun glued to the kanji after it (物干し台), and 前もって連絡する read as one compound. Adverb endings (て, で, に, と, ず, か, も, ば) now stand alone like verb and adjective endings.
- **A note whose drafts were all rejected goes to the agent**: once the review had rejected a field three times, `plans generate` skipped the note (`GENERATION_ATTEMPTS_EXHAUSTED`) and the campaign took that as done, so the note was approved without the field. Such a note now waits for the agent (`GENERATION_ATTEMPTS_EXHAUSTED`: defer it, mark it settled to keep it as it is, or `campaign reopen --fresh`).
- **Notes Anki refused to read are read again**: batches 364-393 left out 79 notes as `PREPARE_FAILED: SOURCE_CAPTURE_TRANSIENT (ANKI_ACTION_REJECTED: notesInfo)` on their first prepare run, while AnkiConnect was under load. The campaign read again only timeouts and connection failures; `SOURCE_CAPTURE_TRANSIENT` and `SOURCE_READ_RATE_LIMITED`, which the prepare job already marks retry-eligible, are now read again too (after five minutes, three times) before a note is left out.
- **A polite お/ご reading is not a second word**: 意見 (369) listed its readings as いけん and ごいけん, and validation asked for a split (`VOCAB_SPLIT_REQUIRED`), holding the batch for the agent. A reading that only adds お or ご to another of the note's readings now counts as the same word.
- **Approve reviews a changed plan first**: a revision written after the review (278: usages redrafted by hand while the batch was ready) brought issues the review never saw, and `campaign approve` left those notes out. Approve now sends such a batch back to review (`CAMPAIGN_BATCH_CHANGED`) and approves once it is ready again.
- **A split unit with an open review no longer pauses the campaign**: `campaign approve` left a split unit with open review issues (277 全く〜ない, redrafted after the review) out of the approval but still approved its group, and the group's apply was refused with `APPLY_APPROVAL_MISSING`, pausing the campaign. The group's note is now left out at approval; an apply refused for the same reason leaves the note out and goes on.
- **Quoted source on grammar cards**: the 〜気味 usage note said "the source notes that it is less common in speech"; the learner sees no source. Drafts that say "the source notes", "the source says", "the source states" or "according to the source" are rejected (`GENERATION_PROMPT_WORDING`).
- **Prompt wording on grammar cards**: the 全く〜ない usage note said "as in the authored example describing a new plan", quoting the grammar prompt's "authored examples". Drafts are now also rejected (`GENERATION_PROMPT_WORDING`) for "authored", "accepted pattern" and "source meaning", for every purpose.
- **Sense choice from a full dictionary screenshot**: 遠い got "far (from something else in quality, degree, etc.)" instead of "far; distant". Its picture was a Jisho screenshot listing all six senses, and the policy picked the sense whose definition shared the most words with the screenshot text. That favours the longest definition whenever the screenshot shows the whole entry. Senses are now ranked by coverage, the share of the sense's own words the screenshot shows, and when every sense is equally covered the dictionary's first sense is taken.
- **A counter reading takes the counter sense**: 杯 read はい took Jisho's first はい sense, "sake cup", which Jisho also lists under さかずき, so its examples read さかずき instead of the counter for cupfuls. A one-kanji word whose first sense another reading shares now takes its reading's counter sense when there is one. A first fix that dropped any shared sense gave 兄 あに "brother-in-law" (its "older brother" is shared with にい and けい) and was narrowed the same day.
- **One agent decision no longer pauses a small batch**: in a batch of three (343, 唄: the agent chose the role of one uncertain image), one decision was already 33%, over `agent_share_max` (30%), and the approval paused the campaign. One agent decision is now allowed in a batch of three or more; in a batch of two it is still half and pauses.
- **Careful mode speeds up again by itself**: after a fast cap, careful mode kept the campaign at one batch at a time for the whole run, even with most of the Codex budget left. While the driver runs, it now judges the Codex window's pace every 15 minutes and steps the prepare limits up (`1 1`, `2 1`, `4 2`, `8 3`, then the saved limits) as far as the budget left until the reset allows, or one step down when it spends faster. At the saved limits careful mode ends.
- **Careful mode after a fast cap**: a resume at 06:00 hit the Codex cap 8 minutes later (its own five-hour window was still 68% used), with up to 12 batches in flight. A drafting cap within 30 minutes of a start now puts the session in careful mode: the next `start` tunes the campaign to prepare one batch at a time and keeps the earlier limits. The first start after a run that lasted 2 hours before the cap restores them.
- **`campaign status` counts written grammar notes**: `applied_notes` counted only apply-job commits, so grammar batches written as split units (batch 274: 10 notes) left the count unchanged. Each applied split group now counts too.
- **Session heartbeat and run policy**: the session driver is detached, so it kept running after the agent session that started it was gone. `start` now touches a heartbeat, the agent session's 15-minute check runs `scripts/campaign-session.sh heartbeat`, and the driver wraps up after `HEARTBEAT_SECONDS` (default 2700) without one. It is also bound to the Claude Code process that ran `start`: it wraps up within about two minutes of that session closing. `status` reports a driver that runs an older copy of the script (`OUTDATED`). `start` run from a Claude Code session starts the resume waiter itself, bound to that session (a newer session's `start` takes it over), so every session that starts the campaign also resumes it. The waiter loops: after every automatic wrap-up for a cooldown it waits for the reset and starts the session again, never after the user's `wrap-up` (which also ends it). It resumes only while the Claude Code session that launched it is open: it refuses to run without a `claude` process among its ancestors, gives up within a minute of that process ending, and needs a fresh heartbeat before it starts the session. `scripts/campaign-session.sh wait-change` returns when the driver stops or starts, so the agent session switches its 15-minute check off while the campaign is wrapped up and on again after it resumes. The resume waiter therefore no longer needs a heartbeat; its bound Claude Code process proves the session. The full session run policy is in docs/cli/operations/campaign.md, "Session run policy".
- **Idle sessions wrap up, and an agent session resumes them**: with Codex at its window cap the session driver ran empty ticks for hours instead of wrapping up, because two batches were `ready` for the agent's approval and a ready batch counted as work left to write. Only approved, applying, verifying and merging batches hold the session now. The new `scripts/campaign-session.sh resume-when-ready` waits for the wrap-up and the end of the drafting cooldown, then starts the session again. An agent session runs it in its background, so a campaign resumes on its own only while that session is alive (still no timers). It resumes only a wrapped-up campaign.
- **Generation renders the other items again**: the redraft of grammar batch 274 failed with `RENDERED_DOCUMENT_STALE`. A drafted item went into a new revision with the other items' renders copied unchanged, and those renders were made before the renderer changed (WP-28). The store refuses stale renders, so no plan frozen before a renderer change could be drafted again. Generation now renders every ready item again when it writes a revision, as `regenerate` and `classify` already do.
- **Undrafted batches are drafted again one at a time**: the 30-minute redraft drafted every held grammar batch together (274-279, 10-50 split units each), so all six competed for one Codex window and the agent had to review them all at once. A batch held only by undrafted items is now drafted again only when no lower-numbered `needs_agent` batch still waits for its own redraft. The lowest such batch goes first; the next one starts once it leaves review.
- **A failed `plans generate` no longer reads as "nothing new"**: grammar batches 274-279 stayed held after the redraft fix. The review step treated a `plans generate` that failed as a whole command (no JSON, an error that is not an outage) like a run that drafted nothing, so the batch went back to waiting and spent its one redraft for that revision. Such a failure is now a step failure with its error recorded (`GENERATION_BASE_CONFLICT`, a plan changed by an agent decision meanwhile, reviews again instead). A batch held only by undrafted items is now reviewed again at most every 30 minutes, not once per revision, so a redraft lost to an outage is tried again.
- **Sessions resume any pause**: `scripts/campaign-session.sh start` refused a campaign paused for anything but a wrap-up or a Codex limit ("look at it, then run: campaign resume"); it now resumes it, since the operator starts again after fixing the cause. The `campaign-tick` skill lets the agent fix a pause's cause (defer, `campaign reopen`, `campaign requeue`) and resume; it still never closes, cancels, rolls back or restores. `scripts/campaign-agent.sh` no longer denies `campaign resume` or `campaign requeue`.
- **`campaign resume` without `--by-user`**: resuming a paused campaign needed a terminal or `--by-user` (`CAMPAIGN_RESUME_BY_USER_REQUIRED`), so an agent driving a session for the user could not resume one after fixing the cause; the user had to type the command. Resume now works from any caller. `--by-user` is still accepted and does nothing; `scripts/campaign-session.sh` no longer passes it. The unattended agent wrapper still never resumes, and `campaign tune --agent-share-max` keeps its `--by-user` guard.
- **`campaign reopen` on an apply that wrote nothing**: batch 280 paused the campaign with `SPLIT_PREFLIGHT_FAILED: … APPLY_TARGET_DECK_UNCONFIGURED`. A `plans generate --use-current-settings` run by hand without the campaign source's `--set` flags had frozen a revision without `purposes.japanese_grammar.target_deck`, and that revision was approved. Its first split group failed before writing anything, but the batch was `applying`, and `campaign reopen` accepted only an `approved` batch, so the only way on was to cancel. Reopen now also takes an `applying` batch with no apply job, no follow-up intent, no split group done and no note committed, and clears its step failures.
- **Campaign batches stuck on undrafted units**: grammar batches 274–280 of the live campaign sat in `needs_agent` for a day with only `PLAN_NOT_READY`, although every note was settled. Their split units still had empty meanings (`REQUIRED_CONTENT`): the review rounds had run out before generation drafted every unit, and a `needs_agent` batch is reviewed again only on a newer plan revision or a met need, so nothing drafted them and no agent decision could. Such a batch, held only by `PLAN_NOT_READY` with an active item's required content empty, is now reviewed once more per revision, which drafts the missing units; once per revision, so a draft that never comes cannot loop.
- **`campaign item --settled` retries failed drafts**: a note whose drafts failed (`GENERATION_FAILED`, for example after repeated `AGENT_TIMEOUT`) stayed failed after the agent settled it, so the batch could never become ready; the only ways out were a defer or `campaign reopen --fresh`. Settling a note now clears its draft failures, so the next tick drafts it again. The `GENERATION_FAILED` hint names all three choices.
- **Part-of-speech tags from a label table (WP-27)**: every dictionary label now goes through `resources/taxonomy/labels-v1.tsv`, which gives it one role (part of speech, register, region, field, script, origin, note or name). Only part-of-speech labels make `lab::pos::<class>` tags, from ten classes grouped from Universal Dependencies (noun, verb, adjective, adverb, pronoun, function, number, expression, affix, interjection), with subclasses such as `lab::pos::verb::godan-ru`. Labels such as `Wikipedia definition` or `Computing` were tagged `lab::pos::wikipedia-definition` and `lab::pos::computing`; they now become `lab::field::computing` or no tag. The other facts get their own tags: `lab::pos-also::<class>` (the word's other uses, such as `issue` as a verb), `lab::register::*`, `lab::region::*`, `lab::field::*`, `lab::script::kana`, `lab::origin::wasei` or `gairaigo`, and `lab::note::*`. A Jisho sense with no labels takes those of the sense before it; a word whose sense has none takes another entry of the same form; a card with no class is tagged `lab::pos::unknown`. A grammar card takes the class of the slot its pattern attaches to (`V て ください` is a verb, `N1 は N2 です` a noun, `S1 が、S2` a function word) and `lab::form::<form>` for the form it requires (`te`, `nai`, `masu`). Apply owns the `lab::` namespace: it drops a `lab::` tag the renderer no longer derives and keeps the user's own tags.
- **Part-of-speech colours (WP-27)**: the vocabulary and grammar cards take their accent colour from the `lab::pos::<class>` tag (the templates put `data-tags="{{Tags}}"` on the card root): one colour per class, with a light and a dark tone, tested for WCAG AA contrast against the panel in both modes and for colour-vision deficiencies on noun/verb, noun/adjective and verb/adverb. In the Meaning panel each sense and part-of-speech chip carries its class colour, and a sense of another class than the card's (`issue` as a verb on a noun card) gets a border in its own colour. Existing note types are updated with `models update`.
- **`models update PURPOSE [--apply]` (WP-27)**: rewrites the templates and CSS of a managed note type in place, with the same fields and template names, so no full sync is needed. Only a note type at a known earlier manifest of that model is updated (`MODEL_UPDATE_UNKNOWN_PRIOR` otherwise: a note type edited outside the bridge is never overwritten). It runs under a collection checkpoint and a journal, and a lost response is settled by `recover reconcile`. The preview says `current`, `update`, `unknown_prior` or `absent`. Writes need companion 0.6.0, which adds the `update_model` variant; companions 0.5.0 and 0.6.0 are both accepted.
- **`notes retag [NOTE_ID...] [--all] [--apply]` (WP-27)**: re-renders applied notes from their stored documents with the current renderer (part-of-speech colours in the Meaning HTML, the label-table tags, the domain profile) and writes the fields and tags in place, with no LLM call. A note that changed since its latest apply is skipped (`RETAG_NOTE_EDITED`) and never written. The preview lists each change (`--fields` shows each field before and after); `--apply` takes one collection checkpoint and writes each note as a journaled apply operation with an exact `restore_note`, a snapshot and a read-back, so `recover reconcile` and `snapshots restore` work on it. Cards, scheduling, decks and the note type stay.
- **Domains in the enrichment call (WP-27)**: the vocabulary and grammar drafts also choose the card's domain from `domains-v1` (`resources/taxonomy/domains-v1.json`: 459 three-level leaf paths from Threshold 1990 and ISCED-F 2013, and Threshold's communicative functions for grammar), at no extra call. The note stores it as `profile` (`domain`, up to two `secondary` paths, grammar `functions`, and the taxonomy version), validated against the list (`PROFILE_INVALID`) and tagged `lab::domain::<l1>::<l2>::<l3>`, `lab::domain2::*` and `lab::function::*`. The dictionary's field labels and the Cambridge topic are sent as hints. A reply whose domain is not in the list loses the profile, not the draft; a note whose only missing field is the domain is not drafted for it.
- **More of the card profile (WP-27)**: grammar drafts also judge the register (`lab::register::*`), vocabulary drafts the connotation (`lab::connotation::*`) and a Japanese verb's transitivity partner (開く for 開ける, kept only when it shares the kanji stem and its ending is a known pair in the other direction; shown under "Transitivity pair"). Derived without an LLM: `lab::attach::*` (what a grammar pattern attaches to), `lab::cefr::*` (Cambridge now gives each sense its CEFR level), `lab::origin::kango|wago|mixed` (from the readings of each kanji) and a negative connotation for derogatory labels. A vocabulary profile that leaves the dictionary's field label shows `DOMAIN_HINT_IGNORED` in review.
- **Coloured grammar slots (WP-27)**: in a grammar card's formation, `V`, `N` and `い-Adj` take the colour of their class (`V` red, `N` blue). Existing grammar note types are updated with `models update japanese_grammar --apply`.
- **`notes retag` on the real companion**: the first live run wrote nothing. Two checks blocked it: the store re-checked each note's old approval against today's validation (`APPROVAL_CORRUPT`) before recording the operation, and the companion refused the `restore_note` under a plan approval (`BRIDGE_OPERATION_BODY_INVALID`). A retag now reads the approval as recorded (its record digest still checked; apply still re-checks before a new write). It runs under a retag decision of the restore kind that binds that plan approval to the note, its observed state and the exact payload, and the apply receipt accepts that kind.
- **`notes retag` over a long run**: a full-collection run (4,159 notes, about 5 minutes of reads) twice ended with `ANKI_DEPENDENCY_UNAVAILABLE` when one AnkiConnect read dropped its connection. The preview's reads are retried up to 4 times with a growing wait before the run stops. The companion validated every operation in its ledger, parsing each payload, twice per write (`labBegin` and `labMutate`): 2.2 s each at 15,000 operations. The bridge re-hashed the multi-gigabyte collection checkpoint before every note; it now hashes it once per process and re-hashes when the file's inode, size, modification or change time differ. The binary accepts companions 0.5.0 to 0.6.1.

A retag snapshot no longer archives the note's media, and a note whose current render would reference other media files is skipped (`RETAG_MEDIA_CHANGED`) rather than written. Connections to AnkiConnect are no longer pooled: AnkiConnect closes each connection after its response without saying so, and reusing one as it closed was the dropped request that stopped three long runs.

A plural or honorific suffix never joins its noun (子どもたち is 子ども even though JMdict lists it), and a JMdict word marked common with no `nfXX` band (店, まだ: `ichi1` only) ranks at band 24 instead of after every banded word.

Drafts go one at a time while that leaves Ollama first. A campaign waits for the quota only when every provider of the chain hit its usage limit; a fallback that failed otherwise counts as a failed draft.

- **Candidates under one reading need no reading match**: when a note's reading field is filled but holds no kana reading (一人っ子 with `一人っ こ`) and every candidate sense has one reading, the policy chooses as among equal readings (screenshot, then dictionary order); it went to the agent with `SENSE_READING_UNMATCHED`. - **A kana word is its own reading**: the policy matches the sense of a note written in kana alone with no reading or pronunciation (こらえる, やがて) by the word itself; such notes went to the agent with `SENSE_READING_UNMATCHED`.
- **する verb forms find their stem's entry**: a note written メモします, 勉強した or 掃除する with no entry of its own takes the entry of its stem when that entry is labelled a する verb (sense review, templates, rendered meaning, the policy's reading match); it was left with no meaning.

- **A collocation gloss copied from an example translation, or shared by two different phrases, is refused** (`GENERATION_COLLOCATION_GLOSS`; 整う got full sentences as glosses, 血脈が通う and 血脈を流れる both "blood vessel"); a する-verb phrase that goes on to a noun (リセットする機械, "a machine that resets") glossed as the verb with that noun as its object ("to reset a machine") is refused too, and so is a phrase ending in kanji or katakana (a noun phrase: 計算ミス) glossed as a verb ("to make a mistake in calculation"); a longer phrase glossed with the word's own meaning (インスタントラーメンを食べる: "instant ramen") too.

- **An unresolved reconcile is tried again**: `recover reconcile --apply` exit 4 (it ran, the outcome is still unresolved, for example Anki away a moment longer) is a step failure retried by the next step instead of an immediate `CAMPAIGN_PAUSED_UNKNOWN_OUTCOME`, and its outcome (stdout) is named instead of an empty detail.
- **Compounds are not the word**: a noun written with trailing kana is part of a compound when a kanji or katakana noun follows it (物干し台, 物干しロープ for 物干し), and an example whose kanji word appears only inside compounds (球体, 球場 for 球) is rejected.

- **A collocation that is one of the word's own forms is rejected** (遠慮する for 遠慮), as a nuance contrast already was.
- **Agent call budget**: `llm.agents.max_calls_per_window` (0 = none) and `llm.agents.budget_window_seconds` (default 18000): an agent or API that started that many calls in the window is skipped like one at its usage limit until the window frees (a call log under `storage.state_dir/agents/`), so `llm.fallback` drafts meanwhile and a quota shared with other work is never run out.
